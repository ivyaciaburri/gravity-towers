Gravity Towers

Red's Turn

How to Play

  • Players take turns moving one piece or tower one square (diagonals count).
  • Land on any piece (yours, opponent’s, or neutral) to capture it.
  • Captured pieces stack to form towers.
  • Taller towers pull in pieces from farther away, and newly reachable pieces are also pulled in.
  • The player who makes the final capture wins.
  • Hover over a tower to see which squares it can pull pieces from (yellow).
  • Squares you can move to are highlighted in blue.
